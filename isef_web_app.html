Analysis Pipeline
01

CLAHE Preprocessing

Contrast-Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization in L*a*b* color space normalizes lighting and reveals pallor not visible in standard RGB.

02

CNN Feature Extraction

MobileNetV2 / EfficientNetB0 extract spatial color and texture features from the conjunctival region via ImageNet-pretrained weights.

03

Monte Carlo Uncertainty

30 stochastic forward passes with active dropout estimate epistemic uncertainty — high std triggers an uncertain flag for re-review.

04

Grad-CAM Explainability

Gradient-weighted activation maps verify the model attends to clinically valid conjunctival regions, not background artifacts.

Multi-Architecture Comparison

Rigorous head-to-head evaluation of MobileNetV2, EfficientNetB0, and a custom CNN on identical splits with statistical significance testing.

02

MC Dropout Uncertainty

Bayesian approximation via Monte Carlo Dropout (Gal & Ghahramani, 2016) enables per-sample uncertainty scores — flagging unreliable predictions for re-review.

Novel for HS level
03

Grad-CAM Validation

Quantitative focus-quality score measures what % of high-activation pixels overlap with the labeled conjunctival ROI — scientifically validating model attention.

Explainable AI
04

CLAHE + L*a*b* Pipeline

Domain-specific preprocessing using adaptive histogram equalization in perceptual color space isolates pallor signal from lighting noise — a novel contribution for conjunctival analysis.
